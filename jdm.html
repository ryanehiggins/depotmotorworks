From Day One

This is not an add-on. JDM is part of the Depot’s foundation.

Built from inside the community.

The Depot Motorworks is already connected to the JDM community and plans to work directly with importer partners from the beginning. The goal is not to become another generic shop that occasionally works on imported cars. It is to become a trusted local destination for sourcing, ownership, performance, education, and community.
