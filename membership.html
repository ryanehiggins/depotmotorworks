The membership we're building.

The Lounge

A premium members' lounge centered on comfortable seating, good coffee, and a compact wet bar with a sink and under-counter refrigeratorâ€”the kind of space you'd actually want to spend a Saturday morning in. At roughly 25 by 25 feet, the goal is intimate and useful rather than oversized: a place to relax, work, and talk cars with people who care as much as you do.

DIY Detail Bay

A dedicated DIY detailing area planned for the building's drive-through section, giving members a practical place to clean and care for their cars. The concept includes wash capability, vacuum stations, a microfiber and towel supply station, and compressed air for finishing touches.

No lifts. No wrenching. Just a clean, dedicated space to make your car shine.
