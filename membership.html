What's Included

What standard membership is planned to include.

01

The Lounge

A comfortable members' lounge with good coffee, reliable WiFi, and room to relax, work, or talk cars.

02

DIY Detail Bay

Membership includes two DIY detail-bay sessions each year, with additional sessions available at a discounted member rate. The bay will provide a dedicated place to wash, vacuum, and care for your car.

No lifts. No wrenching. Just a clean, dedicated space to make your car shine.

03

Priority Service Scheduling

Members receive priority scheduling when booking eligible Depot shop work.

04

Planned Workspace Amenities

The clubhouse is being planned so members can comfortably get real work done while they are at The Depot.

  • Open lounge seating with WiFi for informal work
  • Quiet space for calls and focused work
  • Meeting and conference-room access as the final facility layout allows

Exact workspace amenities will be finalized with the clubhouse buildout.

05

Community & Experiences

Membership brings enthusiasts together through events, shared knowledge, and time spent around interesting cars.

  • Cars & Coffee gatherings, drives, cruise nights, and destination events
  • Tech nights, project walkthroughs, guest speakers, and partner demonstrations
  • Race watch parties and member-first invitations to selected Depot events
  • Opportunities for members and their cars to be featured in The Depot Dispatch
