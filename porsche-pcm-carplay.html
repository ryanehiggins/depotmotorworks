What the retrofit does

Factory screen. Modern connection.

The factory PCM 3.0 or 3.1 stays in the dash.

This retrofit is for Porsche PCM 3.0 and PCM 3.1, on every Porsche model that has either system. The factory screen stays in the center stack. Wireless Apple CarPlay and wireless Android Auto put maps, music, calls, and messages on the screen Porsche installed, and the dash still looks like the car.

PCM 3.0 and PCM 3.1, on every Porsche that has one.

No. The factory PCM 3.0 or PCM 3.1 screen stays in the dash. Wireless Apple CarPlay and wireless Android Auto run on that screen. Steering-wheel buttons, the factory microphone, the factory amplifier, and satellite radio keep working.

Is it wireless CarPlay and wireless Android Auto?

Yes. PCM 3.0 and PCM 3.1 both get wireless Apple CarPlay and wireless Android Auto.

PCM 3.0 and PCM 3.1, on every Porsche model that has either system. The published 911 (997.2) is a PCM 3.0 retrofit. The published Cayenne uses a standalone Android unit.
