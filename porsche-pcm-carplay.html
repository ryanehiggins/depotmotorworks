What the retrofit does

Factory screen. Modern connection.

The factory PCM stays in the dash.

This is not a dash kit and it is not a tablet on top of the console. The factory PCM screen stays. The buttons and knobs around it stay, where that interface supports them. Wireless Apple CarPlay puts maps, music, calls, and messages on the screen Porsche installed.

Wireless Android Auto is part of the same upgrade where the vehicle and the interface support it. The aim is an OEM look: the center stack should still read as a Porsche after the phone is connected.

PCM 3.1 is the generation named for this work. Wireless CarPlay is integrated into that compatible factory screen. Anything else is checked on the specific car before a price is quoted.

Which cars

PCM 3.1 is confirmed. Every other Porsche is a review, not a promise.

Questions

Short answers before you book.

Does the retrofit replace the Porsche PCM screen?

No. The factory PCM screen stays in the dash. Wireless CarPlay is integrated into that screen on compatible cars, including PCM 3.1. The controls around the screen stay where the interface supports them.

Is it wireless CarPlay, and is Android Auto included?

The integration is wireless Apple CarPlay, and PCM 3.1 is confirmed for that. Wireless Android Auto is part of the upgrade where the vehicle and the interface support it.

Which Porsche PCM systems can you retrofit?

PCM 3.1 is the system named in the current site copy. A 911 (997.2) and a Cayenne are published examples of factory-screen work. Other PCM generations and other models are not listed until they are confirmed, car by car.
