Keep the car. Upgrade the connection.

A clean alternative to a generic aftermarket dashboard.

Many enthusiast cars have interiors that still look right decades later. The goal is not to replace that character—it is to add modern functionality without making the upgrade look out of place.

Where supported, we retain the factory screen or factory-style location and integrate wireless smartphone connectivity. Where the original system cannot support an interface, vehicle-specific radio, display, or adapter solutions may be available.
