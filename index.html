From One Enthusiast to Another

This started with the same passion that brings all of us together.

The Depot Motorworks did not start in a boardroom. It started the way many great automotive projects do—with countless hours in the garage, weekends at local events, the occasional stubborn repair, and the belief that there should be a better place for enthusiasts to gather.

I have spent years wrenching on my own vehicles, chasing down parts, solving problems, attending local shows, and meeting the people who make this hobby so rewarding. You may already know me from a meet, a concours, a weekend drive, or one of the cars I bring out.

I am building the kind of place I have always wanted to visit myself—a trusted shop when your vehicle needs attention and a clubhouse when you simply want to be around people who understand why the vehicle matters.
