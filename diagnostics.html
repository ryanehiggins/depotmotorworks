A Systematic Approach

The code tells us where to look. Testing tells us what failed.

Modern vehicles can set the same fault code for very different underlying causes. Before recommending a repair, we work to verify the complaint and narrow the fault with scan data, electrical testing, component checks and vehicle-specific research.

The Depot Approach

No parts cannon.

Replacing parts based solely on a fault code can get expensive quickly. We would rather spend the time determining why a fault is occurring before recommending a repair.

The Depot has particular experience with Porsche, BMW and Land Rover / Range Rover, along with other European, performance, classic and enthusiast vehicles.

What to Expect

Diagnosis is a process, not a guess.

Step 1

Understand the complaint

When it happens, what changed, what has already been tried, and what the vehicle is telling us.

Step 2

Scan & verify

Read relevant modules, fault history and live data, then verify the symptom when possible.

Step 3

Test the system

Check the components, circuits and conditions that can actually produce the fault.

Step 4

Recommend the next move

Explain what we found and what makes sense to repair, test next, or monitor.
